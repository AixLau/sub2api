  account profit gates, per-turn billing-model selection, and exactly-once
  failed-turn usage recording. Resolve composite aliases before scheduling
  while admission and response identity retain the client's public model.
- Carry upstream account-model ownership checks into the local boolean legacy
  eligibility helper as well as the advanced scheduler. Keep profit checks
  after ordinary account eligibility so the existing veto classification holds.
- Apply the trusted-user policy in the shared local cyber admission stage and
  WebSocket usage stage, not only the upstream handler's direct checks. Keep
  audit evidence and usage accounting, bypass existing session blocks, and do
  not create new session blocks or automatic penalties for trusted users.
  Extend the checker contract and all its test doubles together.
- Keep both the user-ranking visibility audit field and the new risk-control
  allowlist audit field. Omitted allowlists retain their saved value; explicit
  empty lists clear membership, and invalid IDs fail validation.
- Regenerate Wire from the merged providers. Keep local rewards, merchant SSO,
  prompt-audit coordination, and usage services while injecting Claude reset
  credits and the composite route resolver. The reset-credit GET route remains
  in the authenticated admin account route group.
- Preserve the product dashboard's spending ranking, active-user trend,
  retention cohorts, rolling-range refresh, and combined token/cost charts.
  Do not restore the retired upstream Top-12 chart. Retain the upstream
  token/spending metric API, deterministic ranking, and metric-aware cache keys.
- Keep the active-user trend repository contract alongside the expanded
  user-usage metric contract. Preserve local model catalog fields, reasoning
  and service-tier policies while adding Sonnet 5.5 metadata and pricing.
